Guests Are Welcome

Curious about Rotary? Join us for lunch, meet members, and see what our club is like in person.

There is a lunch cost charged by the venue. Let us know you are coming so we can plan for seating.

What to Expect

A Friendly, One‑Hour Lunch

Our weekly meetings are a mix of fellowship, club updates, and engaging speakers on topics that affect our community and the wider world.

Meetings usually last about an hour, which makes them easy to fit into a workday while still staying connected to service and to other professionals in the area.

Typical Agenda

  • Informal conversation as members and guests arrive.
  • Call to order, pledge, and brief club announcements.
  • Guest speaker or program for the week.
  • Closing thought and reminders about upcoming projects and events.
