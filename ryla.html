Transformative Youth Leadership

RYLA brings together students from many high schools for a focused weekend of leadership training, team challenges, and service‑focused discussions.

Exact dates, location, and registration details are provided each year through Rotary District 5280 and participating clubs.

About RYLA

Leadership That Starts with Service

RYLA is Rotary’s flagship leadership experience for young people, combining workshops, outdoor activities, and small‑group discussions led by trained facilitators.

Students explore different leadership styles, practice communication and problem‑solving, and meet peers who are also active in their schools and communities.

What Students Gain

  • Stronger confidence speaking and leading in front of others.
  • Hands‑on practice working in diverse teams.
  • New friendships with students from across the district.
  • Deeper understanding of service and Rotary’s values.

Student & School Info

Eligibility High school students, typically sophomores or juniors, who show leadership potential and good character.
Costs Camp fees are typically covered by sponsoring Rotary clubs so there is no cost to students or families.
How Selection Works Schools and Rotary clubs nominate students based on applications, interviews, or counselor recommendations.
Ask About Nominations

Counselors, advisors, and parents are encouraged to reach out if they know a student who would benefit from RYLA.

Why RYLA Matters

Leadership Beyond the Classroom

Not every student learns leadership from textbooks; RYLA gives them a safe place to try, fail, and grow alongside supportive peers and mentors.

Students often return with renewed energy for service, fresh ideas for their schools, and a stronger sense of what kind of leader they want to become.

How Our Club Participates

  • Nominates and sponsors students from local high schools.
  • Helps with transportation or logistics when needed.
  • Invites RYLA participants to share their experience at a club meeting.

What RYLA Feels Like

A weekend packed with activities, reflection, and new friends.

Team Challenges

Small groups tackle problem‑solving activities that require communication, creativity, and trust.

Workshops & Speakers

Interactive sessions explore topics like goal‑setting, values‑based leadership, and dealing with real‑life challenges.

Connections That Last

Many students stay in touch after camp, support each other, and continue serving through Interact, Rotary, or other programs.
