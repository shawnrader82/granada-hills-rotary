Join Local Leaders Making a Difference

Be part of Granada Hills’ largest Rotary club—neighbors, professionals, and friends working side by side on meaningful service.

Start the Conversation Visit a Lunch Meeting

Who We’re Looking For

We welcome people of all professions and backgrounds who want to give back, build friendships, and live out Service Above Self in everyday life.

What You’ll Experience

  • Weekly lunch meetings with speakers, community updates, and plenty of conversation.
  • Hands‑on service projects like Veterans Park beautification, youth scholarships, and food drives.
  • Connections with fellow business and community leaders across the San Fernando Valley.

What We Ask of Members

  • Participate in club meetings and projects as your schedule allows.
  • Support club and Rotary International dues.
  • Bring your ideas, skills, and enthusiasm to help our community thrive.

Visit a Thursday Lunch

Visiting a meeting is the best way to see if our club is a good fit. You’ll be welcomed as our guest—no obligations, just a chance to get to know us.

When: Thursdays at 12:00 PM
Where: Porter Valley Country Club, Northridge
