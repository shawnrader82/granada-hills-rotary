About Lunch Out of Landfills

Lunch Out of Landfills is a school-based food recovery program that helps students sort uneaten, ready-to-eat items so they can be safely shared instead of thrown away. Working alongside school staff and students, Granada Hills Rotarians set up simple sorting stations in cafeterias and lunch areas.

Milk cartons, whole fruit, sealed snacks, and other unopened items that would have gone into the trash are collected, tracked, and redirected—either back to students on campus or through approved community partners—keeping food out of landfills while supporting families.

Program at a Glance

  • Location: Elementary and middle schools in and around Granada Hills.
  • Project lead: Rotary Club of Granada Hills.
  • Focus areas: food recovery, waste reduction, and student education.
  • Partners: school administrators, cafeteria staff, students, and local waste diversion groups.

How It Works in the Cafeteria

Simple stations, clear signs, and student leadership make it easy to keep reusable food out of the trash.

Sorting Stations

Clearly labeled bins for milk, fruit, sealed snacks, and trash help students quickly place each item where it belongs as they finish lunch.

Student Leaders

Student volunteers help classmates understand what can be saved, turning the project into a hands-on lesson in stewardship and leadership.

Recover & Reuse

Recovered items are tallied and either redistributed on campus or shared through approved partners, depending on school policy.

Tracking Impact

Rotarians help schools track pounds of food diverted from landfill and use that data to refine the program and inspire other campuses.

Why This Project Matters

Less Waste, More Support for Students

Every school day, perfectly good food ends up in the trash because of tight schedules, packaging rules, and habit. At the same time, many families in our community feel the pinch of rising food costs. Lunch Out of Landfills sits at the intersection of those two realities.

By teaching students to sort and recover unused food, we reduce what goes to landfill, cut down on methane-producing waste, and help schools stretch their resources. Just as important, students see firsthand that small actions—like where you put a milk carton—can add up to a big difference.

What Our Club Does

  • Helps schools design and set up simple, student-friendly sorting stations.
  • Provides signs, bins, and tracking tools tailored to each cafeteria.
  • Trains student leaders and staff on what can be safely recovered.
  • Supports data collection so schools can see their impact over time.
  • Shares success stories to encourage other campuses to join in.
Rotary in Action

Our Impact So Far

Results from Pilot Schools

At pilot campuses, Lunch Out of Landfills has helped recover hundreds of pounds of milk, fruit, and sealed snacks that would otherwise have gone into the trash. Students quickly adapt to the sorting routine, and many begin reminding each other to use the stations correctly.

Teachers report that the project reinforces lessons on science, math, and civic responsibility, while cafeteria staff appreciate seeing less waste and cleaner trash streams.

How You Can Help

  • Volunteer at a sorting day: Help set up stations, guide students, and record recovered items during lunch periods.
  • Sponsor supplies: Underwrite bins, signage, scales, and carts that make sorting simple and sustainable.
  • Connect a school: Introduce us to administrators, PTA leaders, or teachers who might want to bring the program to their campus.
  • Share the story: Help spread the word about Lunch Out of Landfills through social media or community events.

If one of these speaks to you, we’d love to talk about how you or your organization can get involved.
