See the World with Rotary

Through Youth Exchange, students experience a new culture from the inside—living with host families, attending local schools, and learning another language.

Specific countries, timelines, and application steps are coordinated through Rotary District 5280 and Rotary International’s Youth Exchange program.

About Youth Exchange

Living as a Local, Not a Tourist

Youth Exchange places students with carefully screened host families and local Rotary clubs so they can experience life, school, and community in another country.

Programs may last a few weeks (short‑term) or almost a full school year (long‑term), depending on the type of exchange and district guidelines.

Benefits for Students

  • Improved language skills and cultural understanding.
  • Increased independence, resilience, and problem‑solving.
  • New friendships around the world.
  • A stronger sense of global citizenship and service.

Why Exchange Matters

Building Peace through Understanding

When young people live in another country, they see beyond headlines and stereotypes and build relationships based on shared experience.

Rotary believes those connections help create a more peaceful, understanding world—one student, one host family, and one community at a time.

How Our Club Participates

  • Helps identify and support local students interested in going abroad.
  • Assists with finding and supporting host families in our area.
  • Connects students with district Youth Exchange chairs and resources.
