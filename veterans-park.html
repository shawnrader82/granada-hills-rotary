Granada Hills Veteran’s Park

About the Project

Veteran’s Park in Granada Hills is a place of reflection, gratitude, and community pride. Our Rotary club partners with local organizations, veterans’ groups, and the City of Los Angeles to keep the park welcoming and well‑maintained for residents and visitors.

From landscaping and cleanup days to improvements around the memorial area, Rotarians donate time, talent, and resources to ensure the park appropriately honors those who have served our country.

Veteran’s Park at a Glance

  • Location: Veteran’s Park, Granada Hills, California.
  • Project lead: Rotary Club of Granada Hills.
  • Focus areas: beautification, maintenance, and honoring local veterans.
  • Partners: City of Los Angeles, local veterans’ organizations, and community volunteers.

A Closer Look at Veteran’s Park

It may be a small corner of Granada Hills, but it carries a lot of meaning.

The Memorial Entrance

The “Welcome to Granada Hills” sign, bronze statue, and plantings greet visitors at the corner of Chatsworth Street and Zelzah Avenue.

Flags & Sponsored Walkway

A short brick walkway, built from sponsored tribute bricks with the names of families, veterans, and local businesses, leads visitors past the service flags and memorial.

Rotary Volunteers

Regular cleanup and planting days keep the small site clean, colorful, and safe so it continues to honor those who served.

Why This Park Matters

Honoring Service Close to Home

Veteran’s Park is a visible reminder of the men and women from our community who served in the armed forces. It is where families gather for ceremonies, students learn about service and sacrifice, and neighbors pause to reflect. When the park looks cared for, it sends a message that Granada Hills remembers and appreciates its veterans.

As one of the most active service clubs in the north San Fernando Valley, our members see this park as a natural place to live out Rotary’s motto of “Service Above Self.” Keeping the grounds clean, safe, and welcoming is one meaningful way we can say thank you.

What Our Club Does at Veteran’s Park

  • Organizes regular cleanup and beautification days.
  • Plants trees, drought‑tolerant landscaping, and seasonal flowers around the memorial areas.
  • Supports basic maintenance such as repainting railings, benches, and curbs when requested.
  • Assists with flag displays and logistics for ceremonies that honor veterans and their families.
  • Invites youth groups and students to participate, turning work days into hands‑on lessons in service.

Our Impact at Veteran’s Park

Recent Projects at the Park

In recent years, Granada Hills Rotarians have hosted multiple work days at Veteran’s Park. Volunteers have planted new landscaping at the entrance, cleaned and mulched planter areas, and refreshed paint on curbs and railings to improve visibility and safety. Members also help prepare the space for community observances such as Memorial Day and Veterans Day.

These projects are simple but meaningful. When veterans and their families arrive for a ceremony and see a clean, well‑kept park, they know that the community still cares about their service and sacrifice.

How You Can Help

  • Join a work day: Help with planting, cleanup, light painting, or setting up for ceremonies.
  • Sponsor supplies: Underwrite plants, mulch, paint, and tools for upcoming projects.
  • Connect us with veterans: Introduce local veterans’ organizations or families we can honor.
  • Bring a youth group: Give students a hands‑on service project they’ll remember.

If one of these speaks to you, we’d be honored to have you involved.
