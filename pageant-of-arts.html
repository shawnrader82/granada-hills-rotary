For High School Students

Granada Hills Rotary sponsors local students to compete in the arts at the club level, with winners advancing to the Rotary District 5280 Pageant of the Arts.

Exact dates, location, and application deadlines are announced each year as the competition approaches.

About the Event

Celebrating Student Creativity

Pageant of the Arts gives high school students a chance to share their talents and compete for recognition and scholarship awards. At the club level, Granada Hills Rotary hosts a local competition and then sponsors selected students to advance to the Rotary District 5280 Pageant of the Arts.

Students gain performance experience, build confidence, and connect with Rotarians and community supporters who value the arts and youth development.

Areas of Competition

  • Art (two‑dimensional or three‑dimensional).
  • Music – instrumental performance.
  • Music – vocal performance.
  • Dance.
  • Speech.

Why This Event Matters

Investing in Young Artists

For many students, Pageant of the Arts is one of the first times they perform or exhibit their work beyond the classroom. The experience helps them grow as artists, communicators, and leaders.

For our club, it is a way to celebrate the creativity in local schools, build relationships with educators, and encourage young people who might not otherwise cross paths with Rotary.

How Our Club Supports Students

  • Works with schools and arts programs to share information about the competition.
  • Organizes local auditions or submissions to select club‑sponsored participants.
  • Provides encouragement and logistical support as students prepare for the district event.
  • Recognizes participants and winners at a club meeting or special reception.
