Investing in Local Students

Granada Hills Rotary awards scholarships based on a blend of academics, service, leadership, and need.

Exact deadlines, award amounts, and application links are confirmed each year as the program opens.

About the Scholarships

Recognizing Service‑Minded Students

Our scholarships support students who combine solid academics with community service, leadership, and good character.

Funds can be used for tuition, books, or other education‑related expenses at accredited colleges, universities, or vocational programs.

What We Look For

  • Consistent community service or volunteer involvement.
  • Leadership at school, work, or in youth programs.
  • Academic effort and growth, not just GPA alone.
  • Clear education and career goals.

Student & School Info

Eligibility High school seniors who live in or attend school in the Granada Hills area.
Award Amounts Specific award amounts and number of scholarships are set each year.
Timeline Applications typically open in early spring with awards announced before graduation.

Why This Matters

Opening Doors After Graduation

Scholarships can be the difference between delaying education and starting on time for many students.

By investing in local students, Rotary helps build the next generation of community‑minded leaders who will give back to Granada Hills and beyond.

How Our Club Helps

  • Works with counselors and schools to share scholarship details.
  • Provides a simple application and clear selection criteria.
  • Invites recipients to a club meeting or event to celebrate their achievements.

Recognition & Support

Scholarship awards are celebrated publicly, and students are encouraged to stay involved with Rotary service.

Get Involved
