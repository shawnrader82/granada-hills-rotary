What to Expect

Tastings, Music, and Street Fair Fun

Oktoberfest is Granada Hills Rotary’s marquee fundraiser, held alongside the Granada Hills Chamber of Commerce annual street fair. Guests purchase a wristband with ten pull tabs, each good for a beer or wine taster poured in the Rotary area.

This is a stand‑up, come‑and‑go event with a DJ taking song requests, plenty of room to mingle, and easy access to the rest of the street fair along Chatsworth Street.

Highlights

  • Wristband entry with ten beer or wine tastings.
  • DJ playing music and taking requests throughout the day.
  • Stand‑up, casual environment where you can come and go with your wristband.
  • Access to street fair vendors for shopping and family activities.
  • Food vendors with bratwurst, hot dogs, turkey legs, and other fair favorites.
  • Men’s and women’s stein‑holding contests for prizes and bragging rights.

Event Schedule

Exact times may adjust a bit each year, but this is how Oktoberfest usually fits into the street fair day.

11:00 AM

Street Fair & Tastings Open

Street fair vendors open along Chatsworth Street and the Rotary Oktoberfest area begins wristband sales and tastings.

Midday

DJ, Tastings, and Vendor Row

The DJ keeps music going while guests use wristband pull tabs for beer or wine tasters, visit vendors, and grab food from booths.

Afternoon

Stein‑Holding Contests

Men’s and women’s stein‑holding competitions challenge contestants to hold a full glass stein straight out in front. The last person still holding wins a prize.

4:00–5:00 PM

Last Call and Wrap‑Up

Final tastings, last songs from the DJ, and a thank‑you to guests, volunteers, the Chamber, and sponsors as the event winds down.

Good to Know

Event FAQ

Is this a family-friendly event?

Oktoberfest is designed primarily for adults, but responsible teens and older kids are welcome when accompanied by an adult. IDs are required for alcohol purchases.

Is there a dress code?

Casual and comfortable is perfect. Festive Bavarian attire is always welcome but not required.

How does this support Rotary?

Proceeds help fund Granada Hills Rotary scholarships, youth leadership programs, community projects, and international Rotary initiatives.
